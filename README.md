## About Me 👋
Hi there! I’m Abtaha Chowdhury, but you can call me Aby. I’m a junior studying Cybersecurity at Florida International University. I’m especially passionate about cybersecurity, security analysis, governance, risk management, IT, digital forensics, and defensive security. I enjoy following a pattern, checking the evidence, and understanding what happened. I’ve worked as a Certified medical assistant and bank teller, where protecting personal information and keeping records accurate were part of my everyday responsibilities. I now also work on AI response evaluation with Handshake. I am driven by the potential of technology to solve meaningful problems, strengthen digital security, and build solutions that create a positive impact on communities and society. I want to use what I learn to give back to my community by helping people protect their information and feel more confident using technology. Here, I’m sharing projects that reflect what I’m interested in, from investigating suspicious logins to putting together risk assessments and security policies. You’ll also find a few things I’ve enjoyed building just for fun!
